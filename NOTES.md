# Notes on the archive

Written in August 2026, when the 2019 files were gathered into this
repository. The paper itself is not corrected here.

## It is hypothetical

The December abstract says so in one sentence: the results are cut
because the report is hypothetical. The August course paper does not
say that as clearly, and reads as if the study had been run. It had
not. There were no animals, no facility sessions, and no volunteer
recordings from the Department of Music. The figure and Table 1 are
part of the assignment, not measurements.

The inferential paragraph leaves the *F* and *p* values as `x.xx`.
That is not a conversion error.
